# First realism witness gates

## CIV-1 — close-camera authored human

Pass only if one legally verified authored human package is imported and proves all of the following on the same exact head:

- source URL, author, license snapshot and local SHA-256 recorded;
- skeleton/skin imports without fatal warnings;
- materials and normal maps are visibly correct;
- no procedural body parts leak through;
- feet touch ground without visible penetration or floating;
- fixed views at 2 m, 5 m and 8 m are captured in the real Midi scene;
- Web and PC remain functional;
- owner verdict is `GARDER`.

Until then: `production_authorized=false` and procedural NPC visuals remain the fallback.

## CAR-1 — close-camera authored civilian car

Pass only if one legally verified generic authored vehicle package is imported and proves:

- source URL, author, license snapshot and local SHA-256 recorded;
- body and wheels import with correct real-world scale/orientation;
- exterior materials are not white/missing;
- existing vehicle movement/collision ownership is untouched;
- fallback procedural vehicle is hidden only after authored validation succeeds;
- fixed views at 2 m, 5 m and 8 m are captured in the real Midi scene;
- Web and PC remain functional;
- owner verdict is `GARDER`.

Until then: `production_authorized=false` and `realistic_european_car_v3` remains the fallback.
