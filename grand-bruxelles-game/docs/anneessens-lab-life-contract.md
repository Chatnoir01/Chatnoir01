# Anneessens LABO life contract

Anneessens remains `LABO`. This lot does not promote geography quality.

Minimum player-testable life for the Anneessens visit:
- civilians > 0;
- moving vehicles > 0;
- player spawn succeeds at the catalog anchor;
- SIGNALER context resolves to `anneessens` / `LABO`;
- if the life runtime fails its minimum counts, the zone visit fails closed instead of reporting `ZONE_VISIT_READY`.

Current bounded LABO budget:
- 10 ambient civilians;
- 6 parked vehicles;
- 4 moving vehicles;
- activity processes only within 150 m of the Anneessens anchor.

The movement corridor is a provisional LABO activity axis derived from the existing Anneessens and Bourse runtime anchors. It is not authoritative lane or sidewalk geometry and must not be treated as such. OSM/UrbIS remain owners of geography.

Promotion remains human-only under the repository-level LABO -> JOUABLE gate.
